Questions about email flows & automation.

Which flows should we build first?

The audit identifies the gaps in your account. Welcome, cart recovery and post-purchase are usually the starting points; the order depends on your traffic, products and existing coverage.

Can you improve flows we already have?

Yes. We review triggers, exclusions, timing, content and performance before deciding what to keep or rebuild. Useful existing work stays.

How do you measure automation performance?

We review flow-attributed revenue in the context of the reporting window, audience and store performance. Attribution is a reporting measure, not a promise of incremental revenue.
