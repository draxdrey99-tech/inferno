# Backlink playbook for infernoemails.com

Written to the structure in `seo-geo-audit-playbook.md` (Phase 7). **Limitation:** no backlink data tool was available, so there is no current referring-domain count, spam check or competitor link gap here. Run the first step below before any outreach. Do not start outreach until the pages it points at are live and have real content.

## Step 0: audit what exists

Pull total backlinks, referring domains and any odd TLD or domain clusters for infernoemails.com and the old WordPress site. If a cluster looks spammy, review it by hand before building anything new. Then pull the backlinks of the three agencies that rank for "Klaviyo email marketing agency". Any directory or publication that links to two of them and not to us is the first outreach list.

## Tier 1: the Klaviyo ecosystem (highest relevance, most overlooked)

- Klaviyo partner directory listing, if the agency qualifies. Confirm the current requirements with Klaviyo.
- Klaviyo community and publications: offer a practical piece, not a pitch.
- Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce expert or partner directories, where eligible.

## Tier 1b: partner link swaps

Source: the "SEO playbook.md" interview. The cheapest relevant links come from businesses that serve the same customers and do not compete with us: Shopify and WooCommerce development agencies, CRO agencies, SMS and loyalty-app providers, ecommerce photographers. Offer a useful page about what they do, plus a link from our site, and ask for a contextual link back from a page about email. Only partner with businesses we would genuinely recommend. Keep the anchors varied and natural.

## Free tool: Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing shows the backlinks of any domain at no cost. Check the referring domains of the top agencies there before paying for a link tool. It also imports Search Console properties, and warns when a site has no links from authoritative pages.

## Tier 2: citable first-party data

Press and newsletter writers link to specific, sourced numbers. The only honest source we have is the audits. After 20 or more audits, publish anonymised findings, for example the share of accounts missing a browse abandonment flow or with no DMARC record. Never publish a figure we cannot back with the underlying audits. Point outreach at one benchmark page, not the home page.

## Tier 3: founder visibility

Podcasts and interviews on ecommerce, DTC and retention. These need a named founder on the site first (see `FOUNDER` in `lib/site.ts`), because a host will not link to an anonymous brand.

## Tier 4: review and directory presence (lowest priority, steady volume)

Clutch, Google Business Profile, and agency directories. Ask real clients for reviews, with their written permission. Off-site reviews carry more weight than anything on our own pages.

## Where outreach should point

Only at pages built to earn the link: `/free-email-audit`, the service pages, the case studies once they exist, and the benchmark page. Not at the home page, the privacy page or the terms.

## What not to do

No purchased links, no link networks, no off-topic guest posting in volume, and no outreach to pages that are thin.

## Tracking

One sheet: target, tier, date contacted, outcome, link URL, date live. Review monthly. Add calendar dates only once someone confirms this phase is starting.
